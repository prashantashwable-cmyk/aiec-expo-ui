The Screen Bible

Every screen in the AIEC platform, described the way the person using it experiences it — in their voice, in their light, at their pixel dimensions. Nine roles. Seventy-five screens. One rule per screen that cannot be broken.

9 roles 75 screens 5 themes 3 languages 5 breakpoints Owner · Mr. Prashant Vasant Wable
How to read this

Three things per screen, and nothing else

The Operating Manual told you what the business does. This document tells your build team where every pixel goes. Each screen card carries exactly three things.

1 · The voice

I speak as the person on that screen — Ravi on his bike, Sunita in a shaft, a builder with ₹8 lakh at stake. If the description doesn't survive being said out loud by a real user, the screen is wrong.

2 · The wireframe

Rendered in that role's actual theme colours. The Rider blocks are white and orange. The Technician blocks are dark slate. Reading this document is also seeing the app.

3 · The pixel table

Every zone with its exact height, every type size, every tap target, every state. A developer should never have to ask "how big?"

The one non-negotiable per screen

Every card ends with a single boxed rule. It is the thing that, if you break it, breaks the business — not the layout. Examples: the Rider's capture flow must finish in under 15 seconds; the Technician's evidence camera must have no gallery button; the Customer's home screen must never show two primary actions.

Part 00 · Foundation

Seventy-five screens cannot be designed one at a time. They are assembled from one grid, five token sets and sixteen components. Get this part right and the rest of the document is mostly arithmetic.

The classification that keeps gamification auditable

Total micro-rewards on a ₹6,15,000 deal come to roughly ₹10,190 against a ₹1,42,710 contribution margin — 7.1%, well above the 2.5% recommendation and near the 4% ceiling. The fix is not to cut rewards; it is to classify correctly. Conversion commissions and completion bonuses are direct cost of sale (they are), and the cap applies only to the discretionary layer: streaks, leaderboards, target bonuses, wastage bonuses. Get this split right in the ledger from day one, or the spend will look uncontrolled to an auditor and to any future investor.
